Questions

Is it free?

Yes. 44 of the best designs play for anyone, no account needed, and a free account gives you the code of the 4 free ones. Pro opens the other 140, the code of all of them, the prompts and the MCP.

What do I get with Pro?

Every design and its code, the rebuild prompts for websites and apps, the MCP for your AI tools, and every design added while you're a member. Cancel any time.

How do I connect it to my AI?

On Pro you get your own address. Paste it into Claude, Cursor, Codex or Claude Code once (the MCP page has the steps for each), then ask for a design in plain words.

Can I use them in client work?

Yes: anything you make, personal or commercial, no credit needed. Just don't resell or share them as a kit of your own.

Will they work in my stack?

Every design is one React file with nothing to install. Websites and app screens also come with a prompt that rebuilds them anywhere, SwiftUI included.

Is it a separate account?

One account for Construct Design and the rest of Construct. Create it here in a minute: your email, a password, and the code we send you.
