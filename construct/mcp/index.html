A stable connection between your codebase and the library. Your AI looks up a design, takes the real code and fits it to your project.

Your own address appears here once you're on Pro. Until then it says YOUR_KEY.

What your AI can do

search_designs
Finds a design from what you said, in any language: “pay button”, “dark landing page”, “schermata onboarding”.
get_design
Puts one design in your project: the React file, its pictures, and for websites and apps the prompt that rebuilds it anywhere, SwiftUI included.
design_guide
The method behind the library, from Web Designer Pro and App Designer Pro: copy, type, colour, layout, motion, and a scored review of what was built.
